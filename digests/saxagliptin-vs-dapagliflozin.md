# Saxagliptin vs. Dapagliflozin — AstraZeneca's Two Type 2 Diabetes Workhorses, Compared

**Quarterly Type 2 Diabetes Therapy Digest — registry-backed follow-up edition.**

Last edition we led with SUNSHINE, AstraZeneca's single-arm cohort study of saxagliptin. This edition follows up on the registry, comparing AstraZeneca's two workhorse type 2 diabetes drugs — **saxagliptin** (the DPP-4 inhibitor, brand name Onglyza) and **dapagliflozin** (the SGLT2 inhibitor, brand name Farxiga) — using completed-trial records pulled directly from ClinicalTrials.gov.

Registry data pulled 2026-10-23; every trial covered in this write-up has its full registry record verified.

## SUNSHINE recap (from last edition)

The SUNSHINE study was tracked down on the registry as **NCT01608724** (AstraZeneca study ID D1680L00008). Confirmation of the record's key fields:

- **Registry identifier:** NCT01608724
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca (lead sponsor, industry class)

SUNSHINE is a **multicenter, single-arm, cohort study** evaluating the efficacy and safety of **saxagliptin 5 mg once daily for 24 weeks** in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus who were treatment-naive or had inadequate glycaemic control on metformin alone.

SUNSHINE's other key registry facts:

- **Phase:** Phase 4 (interventional; non-randomized, single-group, open-label)
- **Enrollment:** 2,165 participants (actual)
- **Start:** November 2012; study completion May 2014 (actual)
- **Primary outcome:** Absolute change from baseline in HbA1c at weeks 6, 12, and 24
- **Sites:** Multicenter study conducted across China

The 2,165-patient, 24-week open-label design makes SUNSHINE a large real-world-style complement to the randomized pivotal program, and it anchors the saxagliptin review below.

## Takeaway: completed-trial activity compared

**Match counts.** The registry currently lists **206 completed studies** of dapagliflozin in type 2 diabetes versus **101 completed studies** of saxagliptin — dapagliflozin's completed-trial footprint runs roughly double saxagliptin's (206 vs. 101).

**Typical enrollment sizes.** Among the five most relevant completed studies for each drug:

- **Saxagliptin:** enrollments range from 74 to 1,136 participants, averaging ~615 participants per study — the top of the range is set by the 1,136-patient Chinese Phase 3 head-to-head and the 755-patient Korean post-marketing surveillance; the remainder sit in the hundreds.
- **Dapagliflozin:** enrollments range from 72 to 2,000 participants, averaging ~679 participants per study — the top of the range is set by the 2,000-patient FOREFRONT real-world evidence study, with the next tier (994, 200, 130, 72) spanning Phase 3 confirmatory work down to pediatric safety.

**Sponsorship mix.** AstraZeneca sponsors 4 of saxagliptin's top 5 completed studies and 3 of dapagliflozin's top 5; the rest are run by independent sponsors (University of São Paulo General Hospital, Daewoong Pharmaceutical Co. LTD., and the Moscow Regional Research and Clinical Institute). Across AstraZeneca's programs, saxagliptin is heavily anchored in randomized pivotal trials (Phases 2–4), while dapagliflozin's top matches mix a large real-world evidence study with a Phase 3 confirmatory trial and retrospective registry work.

**Bottom line:** AstraZeneca's two type 2 diabetes workhorses sit at different points on the registry map — dapagliflozin has a broader completed-trial footprint (206 vs. 101) with top studies up to 2,000 patients, and saxagliptin's registry activity runs more tightly clustered in the hundreds-to-low-thousand range, anchored by the randomized pivotal program culminating in the SUNSHINE cohort record (NCT01608724).
