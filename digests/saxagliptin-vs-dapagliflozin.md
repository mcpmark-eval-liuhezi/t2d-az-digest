# Saxagliptin vs. Dapagliflozin — AstraZeneca's Two Type 2 Diabetes Workhorses, Compared

**Quarterly Type 2 Diabetes Therapy Digest — registry-backed follow-up edition.**

Last edition we led with SUNSHINE, AstraZeneca's single-arm cohort study of saxagliptin. This edition follows up on the registry, comparing AstraZeneca's two workhorse type 2 diabetes drugs — **saxagliptin** (the DPP-4 inhibitor, brand name Onglyza) and **dapagliflozin** (the SGLT2 inhibitor, brand name Farxiga) — using their completed-trial records on ClinicalTrials.gov.

---

## SUNSHINE recap (from last edition)

The SUNSHINE study — confirmed on the registry as **NCT01608724** (AstraZeneca study ID D1680L00008) — is a **multicenter, single-arm, cohort study** evaluating the efficacy and safety of **saxagliptin 5 mg once daily for 24 weeks** in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus who were treatment-naive or inadequately controlled on metformin alone.

Key registry facts:

- **Registry identifier:** NCT01608724
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca (lead sponsor, industry)
- **Phase:** Phase 4 (interventional, non-randomized, single-group assignment, open label)
- **Enrollment:** 2,165 participants (actual)
- **Start:** November 2012; completed May 2014 (actual)
- **Primary outcome:** Absolute change from baseline in HbA1c at weeks 6, 12, and 24
- **Sites:** Multicenter study conducted across China

SUNSHINE's 2,165-patient, 24-week open-label design makes it a large real-world-style complement to the randomized pivotal program, and it anchors this edition's saxagliptin review below.

## Saxagliptin (DPP-4 inhibitor)

**Registry match count: 101 completed studies** of saxagliptin in type 2 diabetes.

The five most relevant completed saxagliptin studies, per the registry's relevance ranking:

### 1. NCT02273050 — Saxagliptin + metformin IR as initial therapy (China)
- **Brief title:** Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Saxagliptin in Combination With Metformin IR Compared to Saxagliptin Monotherapy and to Metformin IR Monotherapy in Drug Naive Chinese Subjects With Type 2 Diabetes Who Have Inadequate Glycaemic Control
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Phase 3
- **Enrollment:** 1,136 (actual)
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **Start year:** 2014 (started December 2014)

Randomized, double-blind, active-controlled Phase 3 trial testing saxagliptin 5 mg + metformin as initial therapy against each monotherapy, with change in HbA1c at week 24 as the primary endpoint.

### 2. NCT02588859 — ONTARGET-India
- **Brief title:** Study to Understand Usage and Effect of Saxagliptin as First Add-On After Metformin in Indian Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Patients
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Not applicable (non-interventional, prospective, observational study)
- **Enrollment:** 1,200
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **Start year:** 2015 (started October 30, 2015)

A multicentre observational study of saxagliptin as the first add-on after metformin in Indian T2DM patients inadequately controlled on metformin alone.

### 3. NCT02243176 — SMART Study
- **Brief title:** 24-Week, Multicenter, Randomized, Parallel-group, Open-label, Active Controlled Phase IV Study to Assess the Efficacy, Safety and Tolerability of Saxagliptin Compared With Acarbose When in Combination With Metformin in Patients With T2D Inadequately Controlled With Metformin Monotherapy
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Phase 4
- **Enrollment:** 689
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **Start year:** 2014 (started September 2014)

An active-controlled Phase IV comparison of saxagliptin versus acarbose, both on a metformin backbone, over 24 weeks.

### 4. NCT01608724 — SUNSHINE
- **Brief title:** The Efficacy and Safety Study in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Phase 4
- **Enrollment:** 2,165 (actual)
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **Start year:** 2012 (started November 2012)

The multicenter, single-arm cohort study of saxagliptin 5 mg once daily for 24 weeks that led our previous edition (full recap above).

### 5. NCT00313313 — Saxagliptin added to a sulfonylurea
- **Brief title:** A Study of Saxagliptin in Subjects With Type 2 Diabetes Who Have Inadequate Blood Sugar Control With Sulfonylureas
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Phase 3
- **Enrollment:** 768
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **Start year:** 2006 (started April 2006)

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial of saxagliptin added to glyburide versus up-titrated glyburide — one of the earliest registration-era saxagliptin trials on the registry.

## Dapagliflozin (SGLT2 inhibitor)

**Registry match count: 206 completed studies** of dapagliflozin in type 2 diabetes.

The five most relevant completed dapagliflozin studies, per the registry's relevance ranking:

### 1. NCT03422263 — PUSH
- **Brief title:** Performance Under SGLT-2-Inhibitors in Humans
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Not applicable (observational, patient-registry, case-control study)
- **Enrollment:** 450 (actual)
- **Sponsor:** Berner Klinik Montana
- **Start year:** 2018 (started January 1, 2018)

An observational registry study of early physical-performance effects of SGLT-2 inhibitors (including dapagliflozin 10 mg/day) in type 2 diabetes, versus control groups, over 28 days.

### 2. NCT03499704 — Dapagliflozin vs. pioglitazone add-on
- **Brief title:** A Study to Evaluate the Effect of add-on Pioglitazone or Dapagliflozin in Participants With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Inadequately Controlled by DPP-4 Inhibitor and Metformin Therapy
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Phase 4
- **Enrollment:** 133
- **Sponsor:** Celltrion Pharm, Inc.
- **Start year:** 2020 (started February 11, 2020)

A multicenter, randomized, open-label, two-arm Phase 4 study comparing add-on pioglitazone versus add-on dapagliflozin in T2DM inadequately controlled on DPP-4 inhibitor plus metformin therapy, with HbA1c change at week 26 as the key measure.

### 3. NCT02971618 — GLORIA-Russia
- **Brief title:** Study With Dapagliflozin
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Not applicable (observational, retrospective, multicenter study)
- **Enrollment:** 929
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **Start year:** 2017 (started February 21, 2017)

A retrospective, multicenter registry study of clinical outcome-variable changes with dapagliflozin treatment in Russian patients with T2D uncontrolled on their current therapy in real clinical practice.

### 4. NCT02460978 — Dapagliflozin as add-on to insulin (T1D)
- **Brief title:** Dapagliflozin Evaluation in Patients With Inadequately Controlled Type 1 Diabetes
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Phase 3
- **Enrollment:** 815
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **Start year:** 2015 (started July 8, 2015)

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group Phase 3 study of dapagliflozin as add-on to insulin. Note: although surfaced by the registry search, this particular record is a **type 1 diabetes** add-on-to-insulin study — flagged here so readers don't over-interpret it as T2D evidence.

### 5. NCT00908271 — Absolute oral bioavailability
- **Brief title:** Study of the Absolute Oral Bioavailability of Dapagliflozin in Healthy Subjects
- **Overall status:** Completed
- **Phase:** Phase 1
- **Enrollment:** 7
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **Start year:** 2009 (started July 2009)

A Phase 1 pharmacokinetic study estimating the absolute oral bioavailability of dapagliflozin in healthy volunteers — an early clinical pharmacology building block of the dapagliflozin program.

## Closing takeaway: comparing the two drugs' completed-trial activity

**Match counts (as reported by the registry):**

| Drug | Class | Completed-study matches in T2D |
|---|---|---|
| Saxagliptin | DPP-4 inhibitor | **101** |
| Dapagliflozin | SGLT2 inhibitor | **206** |

Dapagliflozin's completed-trial footprint is roughly **twice** saxagliptin's (206 vs. 101 completed studies), consistent with its broader and more recent expansion across cardiorenal and insulin-combination indications.

**Typical enrollment sizes (five most relevant completed studies each):**

| Drug | Range | Median | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saxagliptin | 689–2,165 | **1,136** | ~1,192 |
| Dapagliflozin | 7–929 | **450** | ~467 |

Although dapagliflozin dominates on volume, the **saxagliptin studies that the registry ranks most relevant are the larger ones**: every one of the top five saxagliptin trials enrolled at least 689 participants, with a median of 1,136 and a peak of 2,165 (SUNSHINE itself). Dapagliflozin's top five, by contrast, include a 7-person Phase 1 pharmacokinetic study and a 133-person Phase 4 add-on comparison, pulling its median down to 450. Note that dapagliflozin's broader program certainly includes very large outcome trials; they simply do not sit in the top five most relevant records for this specific completed-T2D search.

**Bottom line:** saxagliptin's completed registry activity is concentrated in a smaller number of larger, predominantly AstraZeneca-sponsored efficacy/safety trials in T2D, while dapagliflozin's completed activity is more numerous and more heterogeneous — spanning AstraZeneca-sponsored interventional and observational studies as well as external sponsor and pharmacokinetic work. For a digest focused on head-to-head clinical evidence, SUNSHINE's 2,165-patient cohort remains the single largest completed saxagliptin record, while dapagliflozin's completed-trial breadth (206 records) reflects the class-level momentum behind SGLT2 inhibition.
